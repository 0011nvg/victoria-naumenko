02 / About me

Seeing education from three sides

I'm Victoria Naumenko. I work at a school in Russia — and I build the tools I wish I'd had in everyday teaching.

I'm a deputy head of school, an English teacher of the highest qualification category, a teacher of Russian as a foreign and as an additional language, and a mentor to colleagues. These roles let me see education through the eyes of a learner, a teacher and a school leader.

My projects grow out of real school questions: how to make vocabulary practice engaging, how to help teachers hear each other, and how to make AI a genuinely useful assistant for a teacher.

What I do

  • 01

    Teach

    Russian as a foreign and as an additional language, plus English. I look for ways to make every topic clear and practice accessible to learners with different backgrounds.

  • 02

    Lead

    As a deputy head, I connect school-wide goals with teachers' day-to-day work and help turn plans into clear actions.

  • 03

    Mentor

    I work through difficult situations with colleagues, share experience and help them find their own professional solutions.

  • 04

    Create

    I design educational games and digital tools. I test what AI can do and judge it by how useful it is for teachers and learners.

Three different formats, one goal: to make learning — and teachers' work — clearer and more engaging.

01A role-play game for teachers

PEDAGOG

One school situation, several professional perspectives. At first only two or three roles see the case; then new circumstances emerge and more voices join in — a classmate, a form tutor, a parent, a school psychologist, the leadership team. The game helps teachers hear different positions, separate facts from first impressions and find a solution together.

  • 40real-life school situations
  • 5–6roles in every situation
  • 15·40·90minutes — online or at the table

For teachers, form tutors, mentors and school leaders

The game is in Russian.

02An English vocabulary platform

LEXIGO

In development

Vocabulary from the Spotlight textbooks turned into short, game-based practice. Learners pick a textbook and lesson, then train words in the mode they like: flashcards, multiple choice, spelling, audio dictation, matching and a final test. Teachers can print A4 quizzes.

  • 2–9school years, Spotlight textbooks
  • 10practice modes
  • A4printable quizzes for teachers

For school students and teachers of English

Interface in Russian. An independent project, not an official product of the textbook publisher.
